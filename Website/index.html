Matcher · for tracking
1.6M
A cross-encoder trained from scratch on 21,959 synthetic pairs. In the combined tracker the heuristic decides first, and the matcher decides when the heuristic is unsure. Opt-in with --matcher; the setting it uses is recorded and bound to the weights by SHA-256.

    
Inference in Rust
3e-6
pw-model runs the language model with candle on the CPU. On the same checkpoint as PyTorch, the top five tokens match and the logits differ by at most 3e-6.
